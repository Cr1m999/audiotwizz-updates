Everything on your status comes from your own music.

No streaming account needed. If your player shows up in the Windows media controls, AudioTwizz can read it. Try the presets and accents above to see how the status can look.

Works with most players

VLC, MusicBee, Windows Media Player, foobar2000, Spotify and browsers all report to Windows, so AudioTwizz sees them.

Cover art from your files

Reads tags in mp3, flac, m4a, ogg, opus and wma. No cover inside? It checks the folder, the player, then an online lookup.

Your layout, your words

Arrange title, artist and album with templates, pick a preset, or flip the order to Title - Artist.

Privacy when you need it

Hide your status with Ctrl+Alt+H, while a chosen app is open, in fullscreen, or during quiet hours.

Mini mode and tray

A small always-on-top widget with adjustable opacity, plus a tray icon so it stays out of your way.

Links and accents

Add two buttons of your own to your profile, and pick an accent colour, or let it follow the cover art.

Set up in three steps.

The installer does the heavy lifting, including Python if you don't have it yet.

  1. Run the installer

    It installs into your user folder and adds a Desktop and Start menu shortcut. No admin rights needed.

  2. Add your Discord app

    Create a free application in the Discord Developer Portal and paste its ID into AudioTwizz. Setup links you straight to it.

  3. Press play

    Start any music. Your status appears on Discord within a couple of seconds and follows each song.

Download AudioTwizz.

Questions.

Do I need Python?

The installer looks for a compatible Python and installs one for you if it can't find it. This happens once.

Does it work with Spotify and my browser?

Yes. Anything that reports to the Windows media controls works, and streaming songs get a button that opens the song.

Can I hide my status?

Yes. Press Ctrl+Alt+H, or set it to hide automatically while certain apps run, in fullscreen, or at set quiet hours.

How do I remove it?

Run AudioTwizzUninstall.exe from the download section above.
